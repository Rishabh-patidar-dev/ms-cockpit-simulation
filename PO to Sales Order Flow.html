PO to Sales Order Flow
Millennium Semiconductors · Order-to-Cash PoC

From a PO in Outlook to a confirmed SAP Sales Order

Each purchase order arrives by email with multiple line items. The Business Command Centre reads it with OCR, turns it into a CSV that SAP pulls over HTTPS, and shows the SAP Sales Order number (VBELN) back to the executive.
